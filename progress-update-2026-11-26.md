**Progress update, 26 November**

- A fellow's signed plan now shapes what they actually see in the OS. The workspace map, the Library, the learning questions and Cobi all follow the plan, so someone who joined for go to market or investment is no longer walked through build steps. Anything outside the plan is folded away rather than locked. This is merged and live.
- Started the follow on to that work: each venture can now say which tools it builds with, so tech setup only asks for the stack they actually use, and ventures that are not building a product are not asked at all. The studio also sees on each plan review whether the plan matches the tools the venture picked. This is in review, not live yet.
- The announcement and email sending flow now says where a message is going and who needs to approve it before you press send, instead of leaving that buried. Also cleaned up the email template gallery, cutting twelve near duplicates and adding ten new layouts.
- Sign ups who are not fellows can now be moved to a waitlist from People and access, with an email to let them know and a clear route back in if they later turn out to be a fellow. This closes a gap left by the new sign up screen.
- Walked the team through the onboarding redesign and the investor reading room. Agreed to drop the travel request from onboarding, hide the business summary until it is filled in from past meeting notes, and tighten up the landing and login screens. The reading room is aimed at production and testing next week, which would unblock the fellows currently relying on Papermark.
- Ran another pilot in the AI tool benchmarking work, comparing how one tool setup handles a set task. This feeds the criteria for the benchmarking module the team wants to build after the break.
