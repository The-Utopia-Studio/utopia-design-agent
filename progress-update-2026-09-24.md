## Progress update - 24 September

- The fellow onboarding platform reached the point where it could be opened to real users. Outstanding pull requests were cleared and the team pushed for a production release today, with a few setup items still to settle around domains, environment config and deployment access. Wider rollout is targeted for 29 September.
- Ran the first live onboarding test with a fellow from start to finish. The overall flow held up well and takes under an hour to complete. Main feedback was that the library sections are easy to walk past and some content is hard to read, so there is now a prioritised list of changes to make before more fellows come through.
- Walked a second new fellow through the onboarding form and found that account setup needs to run before someone joins rather than after, and that people can currently skip onboarding and still reach everything. Both are queued to be closed off.
- Found that the banking section of the onboarding form mixes up UK and international payment details, and that the form accepts incomplete answers. Fixing these should remove a lot of avoidable support questions from fellows later on.
- Took over the fellow verification and KYC process as part of the fellows ops handover, including how clearance letters are issued and stored. Looking into whether the provider can send these automatically rather than doing it by hand.
- The product, go to market and investment modules are still in progress. Content updates and refreshed videos are the remaining pieces before they can go out to fellows next week.
