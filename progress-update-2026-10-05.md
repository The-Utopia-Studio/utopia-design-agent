**Progress update, 5 October**

- Shipped the simplified fellow compliance desk. The flow now runs as six clear stages with one consistent email card throughout, the signed agreement is filed automatically from the fellow's Drive folder, and the stipend, compute and travel steps match what was agreed in the 30 September onboarding review. Finance also gets a stipend tracker with an export, and fellows can confirm from their home page when a payment has arrived.
- Added per fellow control over the resource library. Admins can now show or hide any module, submodule or card for one specific fellow. Hidden items drop out of that fellow's library, home cards and roadmap, and cannot be reached by direct link.
- Landed product usage measurement and the new Studio analytics page. Daily active use is now recorded in our own database rather than depending on a third party key, so the numbers hold up in every environment. Admins get a usage view plus an activation funnel showing where fellows drop off during onboarding.
- Turned on session replay with every input and text field masked by default and automatic click capture switched off, so identity documents, bank details and fellow written answers are never recorded.
- Followed up on analytics after reviewing it on staging. Chart colours now come from the brand theme and were checked for colour blindness and contrast, and the activation funnel recalculates when the tab is opened instead of on a timer, so admins always see current numbers.
- Two threads are not fully closed. Analytics still needs a check on staging that events are arriving in the right project, which is held up by production deploys failing on a missing deploy key. The compliance work also needs a few configuration decisions before production, mainly the finance and approver email recipients and a real test of the Drive connection.
