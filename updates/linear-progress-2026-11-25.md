**Progress update, 25 November**

- Shipped the Communications hub to staging. Teams can now write a message once and publish it to email, Slack and the What's new page, with a gallery of ready made designs, a reusable design library, a publishing calendar, campaigns, scheduling and approvals. Also added analytics on what was sent, delivered, opened and clicked, drawn from real records rather than estimates. Shared it at standup and it was well received; Aiden is reviewing it page by page before it goes live this week.
- Made the sending step much clearer before anyone hits publish. The message page now opens with a plain summary of where the message goes, who receives it and when, plus a line naming who can approve it and how they are asked. Built and in review.
- Cleaned up the email template library so the choices actually feel different. Removed twelve designs that repeated another layout and added ten new ones built from our own blocks and imagery. In review.
- Added a way to handle sign ups who are not fellows. They can be moved to a waitlist with an email letting them know, kept out of the main access list, and let in later if they turn out to be a fellow. In review.
- Agreed to hold the Utopia website migration. Moving to the company Vercel and GitHub, adding a CMS and wiring up analytics are all paused until we decide whether we keep one website or two. Picked up a small interim update to the Capital site before Web Summit so we are not misrepresenting the company structure: collapse to a single fund page, link to Studio from the nav, and remove the entity tags. A brand alignment session is being scheduled to settle the wider question.
